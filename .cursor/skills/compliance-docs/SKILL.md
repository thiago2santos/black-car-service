# Compliance Docs

## Use this skill for

- obligation matrices
- compliance calendars
- document control matrices
- audit checkpoints
- LGPD handling summaries
- SST or labor-related control structure
- owner/frequency/trigger mapping

## Do not use this skill for

- definitive legal advice
- persuasive sales content
- general HR process design when compliance is not central

If the main request is operational RH design, the people-focused skill should lead. Use this skill when the core need is obligation mapping, risk visibility, evidentiary control, or owner-frequency-trigger accountability.

## Default workflow

1. Name the regulatory or control scope.
2. Separate rule from operational consequence.
3. For each item, state:
   - obligation or control
   - practical implication
   - impacted process
   - trigger or frequency
   - owner
4. Flag uncertainty and specialist checkpoints.
5. Prefer conservative wording when the risk of overstatement is high.

## Mandatory questions when context is weak

- Which compliance scope matters here: labor, SST, LGPD, contractual, or mixed?
- Is the goal mapping, monitoring, or audit readiness?
- What operation or role is most exposed to the risk?
- Should the output be executive, operational, or checklist-oriented?

## Preferred outputs

### Obligation matrix

```markdown
| Tema | Base ou referencia | Exigencia pratica | Processo impactado | Gatilho ou frequencia | Dono |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

### Audit view

```markdown
## Scope

## Critical controls

## Evidence expected

## Failure risks

## Immediate follow-up actions
```

### Compliance calendar

```markdown
## Diario

## Semanal

## Mensal

## Trimestral

## Anual
```

## Working rules

- Make the risk visible without pretending to close legal interpretation.
- Distinguish official requirement from internal policy choice.
- Prefer owner plus trigger over vague accountability.
- Show where external validation is still required.
- Keep outputs audit-friendly and easy to scan.
- Lead onboarding-related requests only when the user mainly wants mandatory evidence, legal checkpoints, or control mapping.

## Examples

### Example prompts

- "Monte uma matriz minima de obrigacoes de RH, SST e LGPD."
- "Transforme essas exigencias em checkpoints de auditoria interna."
- "Estruture um calendario de compliance documental."

### Example response stance

Do:

- separate obligation from implementation
- identify gaps and validation points
- use conservative operational language

Avoid:

- presenting uncertain legal reading as settled fact
- mixing compliance mapping with broad organizational redesign
