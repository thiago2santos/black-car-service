# Marketing Research

## Use this skill for

- ICP validation
- interview plans and interview guides
- benchmark structure
- hypothesis mapping
- positioning validation
- channel prioritization
- synthesis of evidence vs assumptions

## Do not use this skill for

- final persuasive copy as the main deliverable
- legal interpretation
- HR policy design

If the main deliverable is copy, one-pager text, outreach, or campaign messaging, prefer the content-focused skill instead.

## Default workflow

1. Identify the decision to support.
2. Separate `facts`, `hypotheses`, and `gaps`.
3. Define the smallest useful research cut:
   - audience
   - geography
   - use case
   - decision horizon
4. Build the output around evidence, not intuition.
5. Mark uncertain claims explicitly.

## Mandatory questions when context is weak

Ask only what is necessary:

- What decision should this research support?
- Which segment or audience matters most?
- What is already known vs still assumed?
- Is the goal exploratory, comparative, or validation-oriented?

## Preferred outputs

Choose the lightest structure that fits the request.

### Research brief

```markdown
## Objective

## Decision to support

## Known facts

## Main hypotheses

## Critical gaps

## Recommended next research steps
```

### Interview plan

```markdown
## Target profile

## Questions to validate

## Interview questions

## Signals to watch

## What would confirm or refute the hypothesis
```

### Evidence synthesis

```markdown
## Strong evidence

## Recurring signals

## Open doubts

## Implications for positioning or sales
```

## Working rules

- Prefer practical research over abstract strategy language.
- Translate vague claims into testable questions.
- Keep hypotheses falsifiable.
- Distinguish user, operator, influencer, and decision-maker when relevant.
- If the request jumps straight to conclusion, show what still needs validation.
- If the request questions whether a positioning claim is defensible, treat that as a research and evidence problem first.

## Examples

### Example prompts

- "Estruture um plano de entrevistas para validar o ICP."
- "Compare nossas hipoteses de posicionamento com as lacunas atuais."
- "Transforme esse material em perguntas de descoberta comercial."

### Example response stance

Do:

- say what is known
- say what is inferred
- recommend the next smallest validation step

Avoid:

- presenting hypotheses as established truth
- writing final marketing copy unless the user explicitly asks for it
