# People Ops

## Use this skill for

- HR manuals
- job descriptions
- hiring criteria
- onboarding flows
- training and recycling routines
- performance and feedback cadences
- discipline and occurrence records
- employee document checklists

## Do not use this skill for

- legal interpretation as final authority
- broad marketing strategy
- campaign or copywriting work

If the request depends on labor, SST, or LGPD interpretation, coordinate with the compliance-focused skill and flag where specialist validation is required. If the main artifact is a regulatory matrix or audit control view, the compliance-focused skill should lead.

## Default workflow

1. Identify the operational context:
   - phase of the company
   - role involved
   - risk sensitivity
2. Write for routine execution, not abstract policy.
3. Separate:
   - objective
   - scope
   - responsibilities
   - process
   - cadence
   - records
4. Make ownership explicit.
5. Keep the design lean enough for a small operation to follow.

## Mandatory questions when context is weak

- Which role or process is this for?
- Is the company still in an initial lean phase or already scaling?
- What decision or routine should this document support?
- Is the main concern hiring, operation, conduct, or development?

## Preferred outputs

### Process document

```markdown
## Objective

## Scope

## Responsible roles

## Minimum required steps

## Controls and records

## Cadence
```

### Job description

```markdown
## Purpose of the role

## Main responsibilities

## Mandatory requirements

## Behavioral expectations

## Critical checkpoints
```

### Cadence or checklist

```markdown
## Trigger

## Required actions

## Owner

## Evidence to keep
```

## Working rules

- Prioritize safety, clarity, and operational sustainability.
- Treat the worker as a critical operating asset, not just headcount.
- Avoid routines that assume chronic overload.
- Record what matters for follow-up and accountability.
- Use simple governance that can actually be maintained.
- Lead onboarding when the user needs journey steps, owners, and routines; defer to compliance when the main need is mandatory evidence or regulatory control mapping.

## Examples

### Example prompts

- "Estruture um manual enxuto da area de gestao de pessoas."
- "Reescreva essa descricao de cargo com criterios eliminatorios claros."
- "Monte um fluxo de onboarding para motorista executivo."

### Example response stance

Do:

- define owners and frequencies
- include minimum evidence or records
- keep the process executable by a small team

Avoid:

- pretending legal review is unnecessary
- writing generic HR text disconnected from the operating reality
